Global Tech University - College Admission Information

1. Overview
Global Tech University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, and Arts. 
The academic year is divided into two semesters: Fall (starts in September) and Spring (starts in January).

2. Eligibility Criteria
- Undergraduate (B.S. Computer Science): Minimum high school GPA of 3.2 on a 4.0 scale. SAT score of 1200 or ACT score of 25.
- Undergraduate (B.A. Business): Minimum high school GPA of 3.0. SAT score of 1100 or ACT score of 22.
- Postgraduate (M.S. Computer Science): Bachelor's degree in a related field with a minimum GPA of 3.5. GRE score of 310+.
- International Students: Must provide TOEFL (minimum 90) or IELTS (minimum 6.5) scores to prove English proficiency.

3. Important Deadlines
- Fall Semester Admission: 
  - Early Action: November 1st
  - Regular Decision: January 15th
  - Financial Aid Application: February 15th
- Spring Semester Admission:
  - Regular Decision: October 15th

4. Fee Structure (per semester)
- Tuition Fee (Undergraduate): $15,000
- Tuition Fee (Postgraduate): $18,000
- On-campus Housing & Meal Plan: $6,000
- Technology & Library Fee: $500
- International Student Fee (if applicable): $1,000

5. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Merit-based Scholarships: Awarded to students with a high school GPA of 3.8+ and SAT 1400+. Awards range from $2,000 to $10,000 per year.
- Need-based Grants: Available for domestic students who demonstrate financial need via the FAFSA.
- Graduate Assistantships: Available for M.S. students. Provides a full tuition waiver and a monthly stipend of $1,500 for teaching or research duties.

6. Application Process
Step 1: Submit the online application form via the Global Tech Portal.
Step 2: Pay the non-refundable application fee of $75.
Step 3: Upload official transcripts and standardized test scores.
Step 4: Submit two letters of recommendation and a 500-word personal statement.
Step 5: Track application status online. Decisions for Regular Decision are released by March 31st (Fall) and November 30th (Spring).

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Q: Can I apply without SAT/ACT scores?
A: No, Global Tech University requires standardized test scores for all undergraduate applicants.

Q: Is on-campus housing mandatory for freshmen?
A: Yes, all first-year undergraduate students are required to live on campus.

Q: How can I change my intended major after submitting my application?
A: You must email the admissions office (admissions@globaltech.edu) with your application ID to request a major change before the application deadline.

Q: Are there any fee waivers for the application fee?
A: Yes, fee waivers are available for students who demonstrate severe financial hardship. A letter from a high school counselor is required.

Q: Can international students work on campus?
A: Yes, international students on an F-1 visa can work up to 20 hours per week on campus during the academic semester.
